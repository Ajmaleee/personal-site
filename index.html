about me

hey, i'm ajmal, 17. this is just my little personal page where i keep links to stuff i've built and random things about me.

most of my time goes into arduino and esp32 projects — sensors, dashboards, small automation stuff. and if there's a cat nearby, it's getting my attention whether it wants it or not.

arduino & esp32

pretty much everything i build ends up on an arduino or an esp32. inventory shelves, landslide sensors, digital contact cards, alarm clocks. if it can read a sensor or serve a page from a microcontroller, i've probably tried it.

cats 🐾

don't have one of my own yet but every stray cat in my neighborhood knows my face at this point. one of them basically waits for me near my gate. no name for her officially but i call her Biscuit.
